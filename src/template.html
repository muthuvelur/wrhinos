Birmingham W/Rhinos Cycling Club

W/Rhinos

Freedom through Responsibility. Adventure through Community.

We exist because travelling by bicycle changes people. We organise rides, but organising rides is the method, not the point — the point is what happens to people on them.

Why “W/Rhinos”? It began as an affectionate nickname — the whining rhinos, forever grumbling about hills and headwinds before getting on with it. We write it WRhinos where a form objects to punctuation.

At a glance

The handful of things worth knowing before your first ride. The documents below have the full detail.

Never a race

Speed, distance and climbing are personal achievements, not standing. It’s also an insurance condition — no races, time trials or mass starts.

Framework · Standard One

Challenge with choice

Shorter stages, optional climbs, the van, or joining as a non-rider. Taking an option isn’t failure — it’s the design working.

Framework · Standard One

Riders from age ten

Every rider under 18 is accompanied throughout by a parent, or an adult the parents have named in writing.

Framework · Standard Two

E-bikes welcome

Standard pedelecs (assist to 25 km/h) on every ride. Plan your range and charging; speed pedelecs aren’t suitable.

Framework · Standard Four

Trips run at cost

Deposit on booking, final balance billed after the trip once every cost is known. Plus a flat £50 per rider, given in full to a charity the riders choose.

Framework · Standard Three

Insure yourself

Trips aren’t package holidays, so there’s no package-travel protection. Personal travel insurance is a requirement, not a suggestion.

Framework · Standard Three

The spirit of how we ride: why we exist, the eight commitments of the Ride Charter, ride standards, the Trip Guide and how the club is run.
