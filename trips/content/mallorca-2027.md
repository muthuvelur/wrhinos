## Riding in groups

Sixty riders in one bunch is a road closure, not a group ride, so we split into five pace groups of 10–12 and stagger the starts.

- **Pace groups:** five groups, each with a ride leader and a back marker. We'll sort the groups before the trip, and you can swap after Friday's ride, no questions asked (and only mild teasing).
- **Starts:** fastest group last, so everyone reaches coffee within about half an hour of each other.
- **Support:** two vans, one mechanic van with the Tramuntana groups and one sweep van for the short options and tired legs.
- **Regroup points:** at the top of every major climb and at key junctions, marked on the RideWithGPS cue sheets.
- **Road rules:** Spain allows riding two abreast and drivers must give 1.5 m. Single file on descents, in villages and whenever a coach appears.
- **Sa Calobra:** it's a dead end, so groups descend in turn and the van waits at the top at Coll dels Reis.
- **Formentor:** nobody races the lighthouse descent. There are goats, and the goats are undefeated.

The W/Rhinos Ride Charter and On the Road one-pager apply, as on every ride.

## Getting there

You book your own flights. There are direct flights from Birmingham, Manchester and East Midlands to Palma. Aim to arrive by Thursday evening and leave from mid-afternoon on Monday. We'll arrange coaches from Palma airport to Port de Pollença (about an hour) for the main arrival and departure times.

## Hire bikes

We hire bikes on the island, delivered to the hotel on Thursday evening and collected on Monday lunchtime.

- **Choose your bike** when you sign up. Expect roughly €100–180 for a carbon road bike for the four days, or about €180–200 for a road e-bike. You pay for the bike you choose.
- **Sizing:** we ask for your height, inside leg, usual frame size and saddle height when you sign up, and send them to the hire shops.
- **Bring your own:** pedals, shoes, helmet, your saddle if you're fussy, bike computer and mount, and bottles.
- **Fitting:** an hour on Thursday evening in the hotel bike room to set saddle heights and fit pedals.
- **Bringing your own bike instead?** You can, but you arrange and pay for flying it yourself.

## Where we stay

One cycle-friendly 4-star hotel in Port de Pollença for everyone, half board, four nights. Rooms are mostly twins with a few singles; tell us who you'd like to share with when you sign up. Sharing requests are requests, not promises, and a single room costs more.

## Early-May watch-outs

- **Formentor traffic:** the car restrictions on the Formentor road only start on 15 May, so on our dates it's open to all traffic. Hence the 07:30 start on Monday: we want to be off the headland by 10:30.
- **Mallorca 312:** the big sportive is the weekend before us (Saturday 24 April 2027). If you want to ride it, you'll need to come out a week earlier.
- **Saturday 1 May is Labour Day in Spain.** Many shops close. Cafés in Lluc and Pollença usually open, and the vans carry food and water just in case.
- **Sa Calobra coaches:** tour coaches use the road from mid-morning. Single file on the hairpins and let them through.
- **Weather:** usually low to mid 20s °C on the coast, cooler and sometimes wet or windy up at Lluc and Coll dels Reis. A gilet and arm warmers go in every back pocket.
- **Insurance:** travel insurance that covers cycling is a must, as on every trip.

## How payment works

Like every W/Rhinos trip, this one runs at cost. You pay a non-refundable deposit once the trip is confirmed, an interim payment if the hotel needs money before we go, and the final balance after the trip once every cost is known. A flat £50 per rider goes to a charity the riders choose.
